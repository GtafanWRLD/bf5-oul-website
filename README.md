# BF5 Tools

Drag-and-drop tier lists and gun stats for Battlefield V:

- **Players**: search any BF5 platoon and rank its members (with their EA avatars).
- **Weapons**, **Vehicles**, **Maps**: every one in the game, with in-game pictures.
- **Gun stats**: every gun's datamined stats (time to kill, fire rate, velocity, recoil, spread,
  reload), with an interactive damage chart and the specialization tree. Stats datamined by
  [sym.gg](https://sym.gg).
- **Vehicle upgrades**: every tank and plane's specialization tree and what each upgrade does, to
  plan a build. Upgrade lists from the [Battlefield wiki](https://battlefield.fandom.com/wiki/Specializations_(Battlefield_V)/Vehicles).
- **Sensitivity & FOV**: per-scope zoom sensitivity values for Uniform Soldier Aiming at coefficient 0,
  and an FOV converter that tells you what FOV to set when you change your in-game aspect ratio.
- **Randomizer**: a random loadout for any class (combat role, primary with specializations and sight,
  sidearm, gadgets and throwable) to copy and share. Loadout lists from the Battlefield wiki.
- **Player lookup**: any PC player's BFV stats (or two side by side with Compare), top weapons and vehicles, platoons, other BF games they own,
  the server they're playing on right now (from gametools.network), plus name history and where they were
  last seen from our Discord bot. To refresh that, drag the bot's data/store.json onto tools/update-bot-history.bat.

Add notes to any card (double-click), rename / recolor / reorder tiers, and save a tier list as a PNG.
Everything is saved in your browser's local storage: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Player, platoon and server data comes live from [gametools.network](https://gametools.network).
